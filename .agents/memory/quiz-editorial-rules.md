---
name: Quiz explanation editorial rules
description: When original quiz explanations may be shortened and which wording must be preserved.
---

Quiz explanations whose originals have two sentences or fewer must use the original wording verbatim. Shorten only originals longer than two sentences, without adding facts or changing legal citations.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested restoration of the original wording, including the legal obligation «وعليها التحقق من صحة الولاية», rather than paraphrasing already-short explanations.

**How to apply:** Compare against the original explanation before editing displayed quiz feedback. Count sentence boundaries rather than decimal points in numbers, and preserve source attributions and legal citations.
