---
name: Mobile-first audience
description: The owner's audience context and responsive verification priorities.
---

Most visitors use phones or iPads. Treat phone and tablet reading, navigation and touch interaction as the primary experience, not a reduced desktop layout.

**Why:** The owner explicitly described phones/iPads as the majority of the audience when requesting the mobile-first polish.

**How to apply:** Prioritize narrow-phone widths and iPad portrait/landscape during responsive changes. Verify readable text, reachable controls, magazine width and separation between floating controls before desktop polish.
